# Positioning Framework & Statement · Roamly Groups

## 1. Positioning framework

- **For (target audience):** Friends traveling together who are already browsing Roamly for activities, especially the person organizing the group and the friends they invite.
- **Who want / need:** “We want to find something everyone will enjoy, agree on the plan, and book it without one person having to coordinate everything and pay upfront.”
- **We offer:** Roamly Groups is a group booking experience for Roamly’s curated local activities, combining shared planning, booking, and split payments.
- **Because of (value proposition):** It helps the group turn different preferences and schedules into a confirmed plan, while making the organizer less responsible for chasing decisions and reimbursements.
- **Despite (barriers):** Airbnb has greater brand recognition and a broader marketplace, and already supports private group bookings and group messaging. Some travelers may default to Airbnb, and Roamly still needs to prove that invited friends will complete planning and payment in its new group flow. Airbnb’s private group availability depends on the host, and paid-in-full reservations can’t be split across multiple payment methods.
- **Better than (differentiation):** For friends whose main challenge is coordinating and paying together, Roamly connects the group decision, shared plan, and booking in one journey. Airbnb is strong at discovery and private reservations; Roamly can win when the organizer needs the group to align and pay without managing separate workarounds.

## 2. Positioning statement

### First draft
Roamly Groups lets friends book curated local experiences together, with a shared itinerary and split payments. Everyone can agree on the plan and book without leaving one person to coordinate everything or pay the full amount upfront.

### Pressure test
- **Test I ran:** B · Competitor discomfort
- **What it revealed:** As an Airbnb product leader: I couldn’t use it word for word today. “Curated local experiences” and group bookings overlap with what Airbnb offers, and we have group messaging after a reservation is confirmed. But Airbnb doesn’t let guests split a paid-in-full reservation across multiple payment methods. www.airbnb.com

The most ownable part is that friends can plan and pay together without one person carrying the coordination or the full upfront cost. That distinction holds only if Roamly delivers both in the actual booking flow; otherwise, the statement risks sounding like a promise any competitor could make.

### Final statement
Roamly Groups lets friends plan a shared itinerary around Roamly’s vetted local experiences and book with each person paying their share. The group can confirm plans without leaving one person to coordinate everyone or cover the full cost upfront.
