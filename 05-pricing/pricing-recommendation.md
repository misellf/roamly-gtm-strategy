# Pricing Recommendation · Roamly Groups

## 1. Pricing goal

- **Goal:** Enter a new market

## 2. Pricing model

- **Model:** Transaction-based
- **Who pays, and for what:** Group members pay for their portion of the experience. The organizer shouldn’t have to cover everyone’s cost. Keep creating a plan and inviting friends free so there’s little friction to start a group booking.

## 3. Packaging

- **Tiers:**
  - **Package:** Plan and Invite
  - **Who it's for:** Organizers getting a group started
  - **Rough Price:** Free
  - **Whats included:** Create a group plan, invite friends, and share the itinerary
    
  - **Package:** Book Together
  - **Who it's for:** Most groups ready to book
  - **Rough Price:** $0 Groups fee
  - **Whats included:** Experience price varies by host. Group booking and split payment; each member pays their share of the experience price
    
  - **Package:** Custom Group Experience
  - **Who it's for:** Groups needing a host-defined or larger experience
  - **Rough Price:** Host-priced
  - **Whats included:** No added Groups fee at launch. Explore only after Roamly validates host capacity and demand for customized group bookings

## 4. Assumption to test

- **The one assumption I would test before launch:** Assumption: Existing transaction economics can cover the added payment and support costs of split group bookings without requiring an extra fee that reduces booking completion.

## 5. One-page recommendation

### Roamly Groups Pricing Recommendation

**Recommendation:** Enter the group-booking market with **no additional Roamly Groups fee**. Keep planning and invitations free, and let each traveler pay their share of the host-listed experience price. This supports the acquisition goal: an organizer starts a booking, invites friends, and brings new travelers to Roamly.

#### Pricing Goal and Model

**Goal:** Enter a new group-booking market and acquire new travelers through organizer-led invitations.

**Model:** Transaction-based. Customers pay for a completed experience booking, not a subscription. Roamly earns through its transaction economics on the booking, subject to Finance confirming they cover the added costs of multiple payers.

#### Packages

| Package | Who it’s for | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Plan and Invite** | Organizers getting a group started | **Free** | Create a group plan, invite friends, share an itinerary |
| **Book Together** **— Recommended** | Most groups ready to book | **$0 Groups fee** | Group booking and split payment; each member pays their share of the host-listed price |
| **Custom Group Experience** **— Later option** | Groups needing a larger or host-defined experience | **Host-priced** | Explore after confirming host capacity and demand; no added Groups fee at launch |

The custom option is a future possibility, not a V1 promise. Confirm host availability, terms, and pricing before offering it.

#### What Each Stakeholder Needs to Hear

**Finance**

- The launch price is **$0 added Groups fee** for planning, invitations, and split payment.
- Calculate contribution per completed booking: **Roamly transaction revenue minus payment processing across all members, refunds, chargebacks, and incremental support costs**.
- Check how margin changes as the number of separate payers increases. If it doesn’t hold, test a fee before scaling.

**Sales Leadership**

- This is a **PLG motion**: an organizer starts the booking and invites friends; it is not a sales-led offer.
- The customer pays the host-listed experience price, divided among participating members, with no additional Groups fee.
- Don’t promise availability or quote custom-group pricing until the host confirms capacity and terms. Protect the current individual and small-party booking experience.

**Product Marketing**

- **Organizer:** “Get the group to a plan without coordinating every detail or paying everyone’s share upfront.”
- **Invitee:** “See the itinerary and pay your share to join.”
- Use Roamly’s **12,000+ vetted hosts across 40 cities and four continents** and **3× industry-average repeat booking rate** as proof of existing platform quality. These don’t prove the new group flow works. Don’t use illustrative group-performance claims unless pilot results validate them.

#### Assumption and Research

**Assumption to test:** Existing transaction economics can cover the added payment and support costs without an extra fee that reduces booking completion.

**Best method: Conjoint.** Compare customer choices across no fee, a small per-booking fee, and a per-person fee while holding the experience and group features constant. Conjoint can estimate how fees affect booking preference; a Finance cost model and product pilot are still needed to test actual costs and completion behaviour.
